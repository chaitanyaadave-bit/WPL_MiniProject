Traditional healthcare services often require patients to visit clinics for doctor appointments, medicines, diagnostic reports, bills, and payments, making the process time-consuming and inconvenient.
MediConnect aims to provide a single online platform where registered patients can search and book doctors, access medicines and health packages, view reports and bills, and make payments. The system simplifies healthcare management and provides patients with easy and organized access to healthcare services.
The system allows patients to register and receive a unique Member ID, which can then be used to access various healthcare services. Registered patients can search for doctors based on their specialization, view doctor profiles, and book appointments online. The portal also provides information about different medical specialties such as General Medicine, Dermatology, Dentistry, Pediatrics, Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation, Diabetology, Allergy Care, and Therapy.
